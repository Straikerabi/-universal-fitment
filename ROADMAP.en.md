# Universal Fitment — our roadmap

[Deutsch](ROADMAP.md) · [Open the app](https://straikerabi.github.io/-universal-fitment/) · [Ideas and comments](https://github.com/Straikerabi/-universal-fitment/issues)

## One device. The right parts. Less waste.

We are building Universal Fitment to make finding replacement parts easier to understand: identify the device, check its exact variant, and find relevant spare parts, accessories and consumables. The idea is simple: a repairable device should not be discarded because its replacement part is hard to find.

We are starting with **vacuum cleaners for the German market**. Our long-term aim is an app that helps with many different devices across brands, categories and countries. This roadmap describes our direction. It does not promise release dates or imply that planned features are already available.

## Available today

- A growing vacuum cleaner catalogue covering ten brands, with traceable sources.
- Device and article identifiers, manufacturer links and notes about model variants.
- Consistent part categories with labels, icons and colours.
- Expandable part groups on device pages, with search, filters and price sorting.
- Locally saved devices, shopping lists and backups; downloaded catalogues can also be used offline.

Coverage still varies considerably between brands and models. A listed model does not automatically have a complete parts list. Recorded prices are snapshots; exact fitment and current offers still need checking.

## Next: make the vacuum cleaner catalogue dependable

| Focus | What we want to achieve |
|---|---|
| More real models | Add manufacturer-backed model references while preserving country and production variants. |
| More parts per device | Catalogue spare parts, accessories and consumables with evidence for their model relationships. Samsung, Hoover and existing empty parts lists are priorities. |
| Better coverage | Work towards 100 documented model references and at least 100 physical parts per brand where manufacturer data supports it. Bundles and attachments do not count as extra main devices. |
| Clearer choices | Distinguish nozzles, brush heads, brush rolls, bags, filters and other components; keep missing prices and unresolved fitment visible. |
| Practical help | Add official manuals, installation guidance and repair options. |
| Feedback | Provide a reachable contact channel, structured error reports and regular patch notes. |

## After that: make the right part more useful

- Compare original parts with documented alternatives transparently.
- Make prices, shipping and availability clearer, including their source and last checked date.
- Improve model checks, type-plate search and mobile usability.
- Make recurring consumables and maintenance easier to organise.
- Use feedback from real device checks to improve the catalogue.
- Add clear operator information and a dedicated support contact for public operation.

## Our long-term direction

**More devices.** Start with additional household appliances such as coffee machines, washing machines and dishwashers. Tools and other device categories may follow. Each category needs reliable data and its own validation rules, so expansion will happen step by step.

**More countries.** Additional languages, regional model variants, retailers, currencies and delivery areas. A German offer does not automatically apply to a device or market in another country.

**One tool for many everyday situations.** We want to become a central place to find replacement parts for as many devices as possible, from the initial model check to a clear choice of parts. Data quality, practical value and community feedback will guide what comes next.

## Ideas, tips and comments are welcome

What is missing for your device? Which part category is confusing? Which country or device category should we tackle next?

Tell us in our [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Straikerabi/-universal-fitment/issues). You can suggest improvements and comment on existing contributions there. Posting requires a GitHub account, and contributions are public. Brand, model identifier, part number and a traceable source are helpful. Please do not publish serial numbers, addresses or other personal information.

The contact page in the app helps you prepare a contribution. A dedicated support mailbox and a private delivery channel will follow once the operator's contact address is confirmed.

We document progress in the [patch notes](CHANGELOG.md) and update this roadmap as priorities change. Useful suggestions are welcome — the best next feature solves a real problem.
