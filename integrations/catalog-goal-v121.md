# v1.21.1: ten-brand target, Rowenta references and device budgets

The requested target is ten priority German-market brands and about 100 genuine model references per brand (1,000 target slots). Current manufacturer catalogs are not a sales ranking. The provisional work list is Miele, Bosch, Dyson, AEG, Rowenta, Philips, Siemens, Samsung, Hoover and Vorwerk. Brands outside this list remain in scope for later expansion. Never fabricate missing models or use one brand's surplus to fill another brand's gap. Historical models belong in a repair catalog.

This release adds 169 Rowenta RO/RH model references: 149 grouped references from the German bagged/bagless accessory directories plus 20 additional current device references from the German shop. The observed country and /xxx production versions are aliases, not additional model rows. RO/RH references can still differ in equipment; full Ref. Nr. and each article's suitability remain open. This brings the public catalog to 418 model labels, 429 concrete/reference records, five active brands and 763 unique spare parts/accessories. The ten-brand progress table caps each contribution at 100; currently 349/1,000 slots. This is not 1,000 completed models or comprehensive parts coverage.

24 manufacturer device-price snapshots cover budget (at most EUR 150), middle (over EUR 150 to EUR 350) and premium (over EUR 350). Those are configurable product work bands, not wealth categories or quality scores. Unknown device prices remain visible in the all/unknown filters. Part prices never determine a device budget, and device prices never enter part offers, fitment or the cart. The list supports ascending known device prices with unknowns last. Product views link the observed device URL; prices include VAT but delivery needs a separate shop check. Set bundles and ambiguous models without a visible code were excluded. These are saved source snapshots checked 2026-10-07, not live offers; source cache age can differ.

Four initial original Rowenta articles were captured from their own detail pages: RS-2230000243 (ergonomic handle), SS-2230002948 (foam filter), RS-2230002150 (foam filter), SS-2230002913 (floor brush). Only the visible RO compatibility rows were imported, with model-reference/variant-check status; recommended accessories elsewhere on a page are not compatibility evidence. Robot rows and unobserved expanded table rows are excluded. Article prices are snapshots, all device-specific copies remain `variant_check_required`, and no quote can be selected as a confirmed part cart purchase. Other Rowenta models explicitly link the official accessory finder; zero local articles does not mean zero available manufacturer parts. No complete parts coverage is claimed.

Rowenta shipping is separate: the four captured accessories state free shipping from EUR 30. The charge below that remains unknown. Manufacturer messages distinguish Rowenta Shop and Rowenta Zubehör warehouses and disallow mixing warehouses in one order. No combined universal checkout, automatic external cart fill or payment service is enabled.

The manufacturer instruction finder is linked for every new model. No direct device PDF, verified teardown procedure or installation time is invented. Model review recognises observed RO/RH references and excludes serial labels; identifying a model remains `catalog_reference`, never exact fitment. Rowenta is lazy loaded for detail/global part routes, scan part searches and cold backups. New pack and application URLs use v1.21.1. The core is approximately 1.27 MB, plus an optional approximately 166 KB Rowenta pack; external photos/OCR/PDF resources are additional.
