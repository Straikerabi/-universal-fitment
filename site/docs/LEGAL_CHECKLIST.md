# Legal/compliance checklist (product planning, not legal advice)

Before public release:
- trademark/name clearance in target markets
- imprint/provider information for Germany/EU where applicable
- privacy notice and data minimization review
- consent review for non-essential device storage/tracking
- affiliate/commercial-purpose disclosures
- transparent ranking methodology
- price/total-cost display review
- review authenticity/verification disclosures
- user-generated-content reporting/moderation process before community launch
- copyright/licence review for manuals, images and catalogue data
- product-safety and recall handling policy
- accessibility review
- terms of use and acceptable-use/community rules
